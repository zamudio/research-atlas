# Optional research inquiry taxonomy

## Purpose

This taxonomy organizes inquiry around questions a product needs answered, not famous theorists,
schools, or fashionable labels. A family is a discovery and synthesis aid, not a claim that the
literature has clean boundaries.

## Initial question families

- **Learning and memory mechanisms:** What changes with learning, and what mechanisms plausibly
  explain acquisition, retrieval, consolidation, and updating?
- **Knowledge and skill representation:** What is represented, at what grain, and how can change
  be distinguished from task performance?
- **Assessment and evidence:** Which observations support which claims, under what assumptions?
- **Errors and misconceptions:** How do errors arise, persist, reveal knowledge, or respond to
  instruction?
- **Metacognition and self-regulation:** What can learners monitor or control, and how accurately?
- **Motivation and engagement:** Which contextual or behavioral factors affect initiation,
  persistence, and re-engagement?
- **Attention and cognitive load:** Which task demands compete for limited processing resources?
- **Instructional strategies:** Which instructional actions help, for whom, and under what
  conditions?
- **Practice and sequencing:** How should exposure, retrieval, spacing, interleaving, and
  progression be arranged?
- **Feedback:** What information should be returned, when, in what form, and with what effects?
- **Individual differences:** Which differences reliably moderate outcomes without becoming
  unsupported trait labels?
- **Development and age:** How do capabilities, needs, and constraints vary across development?
- **Expertise:** How do novice and expert performance, representation, and support needs differ?
- **Modality:** When does presentation or response modality change access, processing, or outcomes?
- **Social learning:** How do collaboration, modeling, dialogue, and social context affect learning?
- **Transfer:** When and how does learning apply beyond the trained task or context?
- **Retention and forgetting:** What persists, what decays, and which conditions alter durability?
- **Accessibility and neurodiversity:** Which barriers, accommodations, and design choices affect
  equitable access without inferring diagnoses?
- **Measurement validity:** Do measures justify the interpretations and decisions made from them?

## Use

These categories are optional project guidance. They neither define Atlas entities nor require a
run or output to adopt a taxonomy version. Revise them as research exposes better questions.
