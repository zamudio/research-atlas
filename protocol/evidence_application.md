# Downstream evidence application guidance

Recommendations, design guidance, reports, and prompts are application-layer outputs derived from
selected evidence. They must not rewrite the underlying findings or their uncertainty.

Before suggesting a consumer action, consider relevance, quality, generalizability, observability,
inference reliability, actionability, and the consequences of error. Explain material assumptions.
Project-specific destinations are optional output configuration, not core evidence categories.

Prefer observable signals over unsupported inferred traits. Record confounders and failure costs
when proposing an inference. Scientifically meaningful information need not become per-user state
or a feature in a consumer system.

Recurrence across papers can guide investigation but does not establish causal importance,
measurement validity, or decision necessity. Useful outputs should preserve citations to their
selected Insights and distinguish empirical conclusions from proposed applications.
